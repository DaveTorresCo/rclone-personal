Privacy policy

Personal RClone · Maintained by David Torres · Updated September 29, 2026

Purpose and access

This personal OAuth app lets its owner use rclone on their own computer to list and download Google Drive files they are authorized to access. The configured Google Drive permission is read-only: file contents and metadata may be read, but the app does not request permission to modify or delete Drive files.

Use and storage

Google Drive data is used only to perform the owner’s requested file-listing and download operations. Downloaded files, local logs, and rclone configuration remain on storage controlled by the owner. OAuth tokens are stored in the local rclone configuration and used to authenticate directly with Google. They are not published on this website.

Sharing

The app does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to unrelated third parties. Data is exchanged with Google as needed for the requested operation and saved to the destination chosen by the owner.

Retention and control

Downloaded files and local logs remain until the owner deletes them. The owner can revoke the app’s access in their Google Account’s third-party connections settings and remove stored tokens from the local rclone configuration. Revoking access does not delete files already downloaded.

This website

This static information site has no sign-in, analytics scripts, or forms and does not receive Drive data or OAuth tokens. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process ordinary website request information under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Contact

For questions, contact the maintainer through the project’s GitHub issues. Do not post private files, passwords, or tokens.
