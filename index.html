Personal RClone

This is the information page for David Torres’s personal Google Drive OAuth app, used with the rclone command-line program.

The app enables its owner to list and download files they are authorized to access in Google Drive to their own computer. It uses read-only Google Drive access and is intended only for personal use.

This website does not provide a sign-in service and does not receive Google Drive files or OAuth tokens.
